    THE GOOD DOG'S FIELD GUIDE

    A little jump goes a long way.

    Guide Biscuit the corgi through seven pixel-sized adventures. Find the golden key on each trail, then reach the doghouse to continue. Treats are optional, but being a good dog is not.

    Move
    ← → or A D
    Jump
    space, ↑, or W
    Pause
    esc or P
    Restart adventure
    R while paused
    Mute / unmute
    M or the sound button

    A few trail tips: Hold jump for a higher leap. Hop on grumpy critters to bounce off them. Orange flags save your place if you take a tumble. Each new trail restores one heart. Grab a bone treat for an extra heart, a lightning badge for the zoomies, and a squeaky toy to make nearby critters freeze in fright. In the finale, the Mega-Vac guards the last golden key: dodge its charge from a ledge, then stomp its lid three times while it's dizzy.

    On a phone or tablet? Use the paw buttons under the game. Hold a direction paw and tap JUMP with your other thumb, or slide your thumb from one paw to the other without lifting it.
