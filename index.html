THE GOOD DOG'S FIELD GUIDE

A little jump goes a long way.

Guide Biscuit the corgi through three pixel-sized adventures. Find the golden key on each trail, then reach the doghouse to continue. Treats are optional, but being a good dog is not.

Move
← → or A D
Jump
space, ↑, or W
Pause
esc or P
Restart adventure
R while paused

A few trail tips: Hold jump for a higher leap. Hop on grumpy critters to bounce off them. Orange flags save your place if you take a tumble. Each new trail restores one heart.

On a phone or tablet? Use the on-screen arrows and jump button. You can hold a direction and jump at the same time.
